    Data Analyst

    • Job Type Full Time
    • Qualification BA/BSc/HND
    • Experience 2 - 3 years
    • Location Lagos
    • Job Field Data, Business Analysis and AI 

    Job Summary

    • We are seeking a detail-oriented Data Analyst to collect, analyse, and interpret business data to support decision-making, improve operational efficiency, and drive business growth.
    • The ideal candidate will transform sales, inventory, customer, and operational data into actionable insights.
    • Relevant experience in industrial tools organization.

    Key Responsibilities

    • Collect, clean, analyse, and interpret sales, inventory, and operational data.
    • Develop dashboards and reports to monitor sales performance, stock movement, revenue, and business trends.
    • Use Microsoft Excel, Power BI, and other analytical tools to generate actionable insights.
    • Track key performance indicators (KPIs) and identify trends, gaps, and opportunities for improvement.
    • Analyse customer purchasing patterns, product performance, and sales team productivity.
    • Support management with data-driven forecasts and recommendations.
    • Maintain accurate databases and ensure data integrity across reporting systems.
    • Collaborate with Sales, Finance, and Operations teams to support business objectives.

    Requirements

    • Bachelor's Degree in Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, Economics, or a related field.
    • 2–3 years of relevant experience in data analysis or business reporting.
    • Relevant experience in industrial tools organization.
    • Strong proficiency in Microsoft Excel; Power BI experience is highly desirable.
    • Knowledge of SQL and database management is an advantage.
    • Strong analytical, numerical, and problem-solving skills.
    • Excellent communication and attention to detail.
